WHY THIS RESULT
    SOURCES

    A source rating applies to the claim in its original context. A visible caption check covers only what the photo appears to show.

    MODEL NOTE✳

    What the result covers

    For a public claim, inspect the source passage and date. For a caption, the result describes what the models could identify in the uploaded photo.

    Source evidenceVisual evidence
    A CLEARER WAY TO REVIEW

    Three signals. One careful read.

    The system organizes clues for a person to inspect; it does not replace independent fact-checking.

    01

    Encode both sides

    An object detector locates common objects. ViLT answers visual questions and BLIP compares the complete caption with the photo.

    02

    Estimate a class

    For factual claims, the app looks for a published review or compares the wording against reference passages.

    03

    Keep context in view

    Open the source and check the wording and date. The original Fakeddit classifier is available in the check menu.
